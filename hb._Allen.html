How to read this page

Filled means the field holds anything other than an empty or whitespace-only string. Placeholders such as “s.n.” or “[data not captured]”, and codes such as recordSecurity “0”, count as filled.

% of records: share of this herbarium’s records that are filled.

Most repeated value: the value held by the most records (cut to 60 characters; on a tie, one of the tied values), with its count and its share of the filled records. Where no value occurs more than once, the row says “no value repeated”. Where fewer than 20 records are filled, it also shows one of the values, marked “for example”: it is not chosen as typical.

◆ marks fields that the portal fills, derives or defaults for some or all records (from the collection’s settings, the taxonomic thesaurus, the date, or the system itself). Values there say little about the herbarium’s own entry work.

Field names on this page are the names used in CLH’s public download (Darwin Core terms). The portal’s own pages and its editor use different labels for some of them: what the portal shows as “Collector” is recordedBy here, “Number” is recordNumber, “Additional Collectors” is associatedCollectors, “Determiner” is identifiedBy, and “Date” is eventDate. These labels are translations, and the fit is not always exact. eventDate is commonly read as the date of collection, but in some records it can be shown to hold another date. Some fields are filled by the portal rather than by the herbarium, and some values in the download are combined from several fields in the editor.
