What each herbarium's data look like in the Consortium of Lichen Herbaria

CLH field canvass. Snapshot clh_2026_08, the public download of the whole portal, August 2026. Index made 2026-10-31.

Each page below describes one herbarium's records as they appear in the portal's public download: which fields hold values, how often, and which values are most repeated. The pages describe; they do not grade. A value that looks unusual from outside may be the best answer to a constraint that cannot be seen from here, and the people who know why are the herbarium's own curators.

Codes are the institutionCode values found in the download. Codes beginning "hb." are personal herbaria. Some codes hold very few records; their pages are short but describe exactly what is there.

Not included: institutionCode "Consortium", which is not a herbarium, and codes that are comma-separated lists of herbaria. The code IIBYT (66 records) also has no page, because its name differs from IIByT only in capital letters, which the file names here cannot keep apart.

An overflight of the corpus. One interactive picture of how the 83 largest herbaria fill the 106 fields: each square is one herbarium's use of one field, and pointing at a square shows what it holds. It shows patterns across herbaria at a glance; for any one herbarium, its own page below is clearer. Two arrangements: herbaria ordered by how fully they fill their fields, and herbaria ordered by size. (Large pages, a few seconds to draw.)

Prepared by Alan Peterson, an independent researcher, from the public download. Not affiliated with CLH or Symbiota.
